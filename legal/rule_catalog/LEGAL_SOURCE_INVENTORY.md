## Key correction to the earlier inventory

The earlier version was too aggressive in treating file names as legal certainty. The corrected version keeps the following important distinctions:
- Official Gazette notifications and amendments are treated as legal sources only when the actual PDF content shows the Gazette header and legal rule text.
- Section-level packets like `8(i)` through `8(xiii)` are treated as section extracts or applicability references, not as independent rulebooks.
- Advisory, guideline, and SOP PDFs are clearly separated from executable legal sources.
- Unreadable PDFs remain `Needs verification`; they are not converted into executable source status without authoritatively recoverable content.
