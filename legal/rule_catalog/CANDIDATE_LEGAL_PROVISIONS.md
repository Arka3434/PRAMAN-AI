# Candidate Legal Provisions

This file extracts candidate compliance provisions from the executable or potentially executable PCR sources identified in `legal/source_documents/` and the corrected inventory in `legal/rule_catalog/LEGAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md`.

Scope note:
- This is documentation only; it does not implement a rule engine or compliance logic.
- The list prioritizes general packaged-commodity declaration obligations that PRAMAN can realistically verify from package images.
- Where the legal source text is visible and clear, the candidate is marked as verified from the source document.
- Where a title, date, or exact legal status remains uncertain, the field is marked `Needs verification`.
- Advisory, guideline, and SOP documents are not treated as statutory rules unless the source itself clearly states that status.

## Candidate provisions selected for immediate PRAMAN image-verification use

The following are the highest-confidence, image-verifiable candidates for near-term PRAMAN extraction and validation:

1. `PCR-001` — manufacturer / packer / importer name and address
2. `PCR-002` — country of origin / manufacture / assembly
3. `PCR-003` — commodity common or generic name
4. `PCR-004` — net quantity and unit
5. `PCR-005` — month and year of manufacture / pre-packing / import
6. `PCR-006` — best-before / use-by date if applicable
7. `PCR-007` — MRP / retail sale price
8. `PCR-008` — principal display panel placement

These are the most practical provisions for a package-image compliance capture workflow because they can be checked from OCR results and package photographs without needing external legal interpretation or non-image sources.

- `PCR_2011_CONSOLIDATED.pdf` is the strongest executable source for the general packaged-commodity requirements.
- `GSR226_1732871458.pdf` and related official notifications appear to amend or update the rule family, but the exact effect on each sub-rule should be confirmed in the specific amendment text when building a production compliance engine.
- `advisory_pcr(1)_0 (1)_1732860898.pdf`, `LM_Advisory_for_Readymade_Garments_0_1732710356.pdf`, and `2023.12.29 Standard Operating Procedure for Edible oil & Fats Net Quantity Measurement signed copy_1732872010.pdf` are not treated as statutory rule sources here unless the document itself clearly establishes that status.
